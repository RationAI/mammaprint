Exact tile evidence

Each tile contributes w · (tile embedding − training tile mean) / number of tiles. The reference is the model output for the training tile mean, including its bias. It represents typical training embeddings, not healthy tissue. The reference plus all tile contributions reconstructs the raw slide prediction.

Regression uses index units. BCE uses logit units; sigmoid is applied only to the final logit. Positive contributions increase that output relative to the reference; negative contributions decrease it. These explain the frozen model, not biological causation or individual visual features.

Heatmaps use a shared absolute {{ data.scale_percentile }}th-percentile scale across this cohort; the actual scales are listed below. Contributions are divided by the slide's tile count, so large slides may have fainter tiles. Histogram axes vary per slide and use unscaled single-tile outputs. Tile removal and these scores share a signed ranking for a mean + linear model; they are not independent corroborating explanations.
